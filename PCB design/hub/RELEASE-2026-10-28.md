## Remaining release gates — do not hide these

1. **Synchronization:** strict DRC is not entirely clean. There is one generic Netlist Error. Current
   electrical membership matches at every named pin, and native preservation is proven, but the API
   cannot enumerate the exact ECO discrepancy here; the direct PCB netlist call timed out. J3's
   retained library association and old BOM footprint alias are known metadata debt, not proof that
   this is the sole reason for the finding. Inspect the proposed differences manually before any
   full production release; do not apply an ECO that replaces J3's six custom plated slots. No
   schematic import, rule suppression, device standardization or library modification was used.
2. **Real parts / assembly:** verify KCD4 blade fit with a 1:1 print or actual prototype, slot tolerance
   with the fabricator, mechanical support and break-before-make/centre-off behavior. Retain the six
   pin/net mapping in the spec. Confirm the actual microSD module envelope/underside clearance and
   Q1 lead order. The BOM's SS8050 library alias is not the approved user-supplied 2N2222A selection.
3. **Power qualification:** source-track width, GND connectivity and thermal spokes do not prove the
   intended ≈8 A worst-case load safe. Measure voltage drop and temperature, including connectors,
   switch, vias and thermal necks, on the assembled prototype. No switch or PCB current rating is claimed.
4. **Human fabrication review:** inspect JLC's rendered layer/drill/slot preview and the complete PCB
   visually. No order, payment or procurement has been performed.
