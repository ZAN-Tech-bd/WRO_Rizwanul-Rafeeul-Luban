## Verdict

electrical routing blockers were found. This is a prototype layout review, **not unconditional manufacturing approval**:
one silkscreen overlap needs cleanup, physical component fit/pin order still need confirmation, and the exported

## Findings and remaining work

1. **Silkscreen cleanup:** J1's reference text overlaps **HUB CABLE (1:1)**. Move one label before fabrication. This does
   not affect electrical connectivity. The review did not change the user's labels.
2. **Identification:** the team marking is present, but no board name/revision string was found among the live silk
   text. Add an identifier to distinguish this manual layout from the older v0.4 geometry.
3. **Physical checks:** print 1:1 and check the actual upright IR module, cable access, connector orientation and screw
   heads. Confirm the purchased BC557's actual C/B/E order and the active buzzer's polarity. Geometric net checks do
   not validate a different locally purchased transistor/module pin-out.
4. **Fabrication gate outstanding:** export fresh Gerber/drill files and independently inspect them, especially GND
   copper and thermal spokes. No manufacturing files were approved or ordered during this review.
5. **Bring-up still required:** continuity/short tests before power, verify 5V/3V3 rails, then test the sensors, buzzer
   and LED chain per the [station specification](STATION-SPEC.md). Current capability and signal integrity have not
   been physically measured; 1.0 mm power tracks are encouraging but are not a thermal test or regulator/cable audit.

## Important: static ground copper

The existing bridge/pour-engine limitation is handled with static fill primitives and an independent read-back check.
These fills do **not automatically move out of the way of later track, via, pad or outline edits**. Regenerate from a
fresh live snapshot and recheck them after every such change. Restoring old polygons or merely rebuilding native
copper is not a safe substitute. The newly generated writer checks its source geometry immediately before writing
and refuses intervening changes. Historical placement/route/fill scripts do not describe this manual revision.

## Decisions and provenance

- User's manual placement/routing is the baseline; do not redraw or optimize it during a pour/review request.
- Keep the previously requested bottom-only GND policy. No extra GND vias or top copper were added.
- Reuse the existing thermal/fill algorithms, but supply absolute live pad geometry, the shortened outline and moved
  mounting holes. The old layout model would have poured against incorrect geometry.
- Use the absolute pad API's drill state: component-pad proxy drill values differed from absolute pad values in this
  editor. The adapter explicitly rejects the proxy format and unknown geometry rather than silently estimating it.
